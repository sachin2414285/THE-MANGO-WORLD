📚 Knowledge Hub & Guides

Preventive Care

Cultural practices, pruning, sanitation, and planting resistant varieties to reduce disease incidence.

  • Regularly remove fallen leaves & infected parts
  • Ensure proper spacing & airflow
  • Avoid overhead irrigation
  • Maintain soil health with organic compost
  • Monitor humidity and temperature
  • Use certified disease-free planting material
  • Apply balanced nutrition based on soil tests
  • Implement IPM strategies

Spraying Schedule

Recommended sprays for flowering, fruit-set, and monsoon protection.

  1. Pre-flowering — preventives (copper/mancozeb)
  2. At flowering — careful selective fungicides
  3. During rainy season — increased frequency (follow label)
  4. Post-harvest — storage protectants
  5. Winter dormant sprays for overwintering pathogens

Natural Treatments

Neem oil, Trichoderma, Bacillus spp., and organic approaches that help reduce chemical dependency.

Video Tutorial
  • Neem oil spray for insects
  • Trichoderma for soil fungi
  • Bacillus for bacterial control
  • Garlic-chili spray for pests
  • Companion planting with marigolds

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When to spray fungicide?
▸
Preferably during early flowering and fruit-set. Spray in the morning or evening to avoid harming pollinators. Always follow label instructions for intervals and safety.
Are organic medicines effective?
▸
Yes — neem oil, sulfur, and microbial agents work well on many problems and are safer for the environment. They may take longer but reduce chemical residues and resistance buildup.
How to identify anthracnose?
▸
Look for sunken dark lesions on fruits and leaves. Flower blight and fruit rot are common signs. Use the AI detector for confirmation with photos.
How to store treated fruits?
▸
Handle gently, keep at 13-15°C with 85-90% humidity. Use ethylene absorbers and avoid bruising. Maintain hygiene during packing and transport.
Can I mix fungicides?
▸
Mixing is not recommended unless specified on labels; rotating fungicide groups reduces resistance risk. Consult local extension services for safe combinations.
How often to scout orchards?
▸
At least once a week during critical seasons and after rains. Record observations for targeted actions. Use apps or notebooks for tracking.
What is IPM?
▸
Integrated Pest Management: Combining biological, cultural, and chemical methods to minimize pesticide use while maintaining crop health.
How to apply for subsidies?
▸
Visit local agriculture office or NHM portal. Submit land records, crop details, and bank info. Schemes for drip irrigation, organic farming, etc.
Best varieties for export?
▸
Alphonso, Kesar, Dashehari for premium markets. Ensure GI tagging and quality certification for better prices.
What to do in heavy rain?
▸
Avoid spraying, ensure drainage, prune for air flow. Watch for anthracnose and bacterial spot. Use fungicides pre-rain if possible.
How to calculate irrigation?
▸
Based on ETo and crop factor. For mango, 20-40L/tree/week in summer. Use soil moisture probes or tensiometers.
